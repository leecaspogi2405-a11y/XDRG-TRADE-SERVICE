Trading Service Interactive Guide

Step 1: Authenticate or Claim Profile Identity

Access the 'Sign Up / Log In' access portal in the navigation header. Input an email line along with a secure management pass phrase.

Step 2: Post Catalog Inventory Listings

Select the '+ Upload Item' action link. Choose your game module, fill out explicit weight criteria, mutation aura attributes, and values data.

Step 3: Negotiating & Chatting

Click on a user's profile name to send them a direct message, or hit 'Send Trade Offer' to dispatch transaction logs straight to their inbox.

Admin: Add Verification Accounts

Add Gmail addresses to grant them a verified badge globally across the platform.

Admin: Schedule App Update Link

Set a countdown timer. Once the time is reached, the update text and link will be revealed to all users.
